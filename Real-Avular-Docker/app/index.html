Other maps

Localize

Initial pose tool, then drag on the map from where __ROBOT__ really stands, in the direction it faces.

Yellow arrow = initial pose you will send. Works while enabled, as long as __ROBOT__ stands still.

How to localize

Scroll to zoom, right-drag (or Shift+drag) to pan, Center on robot to jump there. Then enable motion and turn in place a little (↶ ↷): __ROBOT__ only refines its estimate while moving. Confirm when the pink laser dots match the walls: at least 40% must lie on map walls (shown as "Laser on walls"; a right pose usually gives 50–80%, a wrong one 10–20%) and "Autopilot position" must say accurate. Furniture that is not on the map lowers the score. Auto-fit searches around the yellow arrow (or the green estimate) for the best laser fit; it only moves the yellow arrow, you still check and send it.
