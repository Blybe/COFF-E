Tips for localizing

Scroll to zoom, right-drag (or Shift+drag) to pan, Center on robot to jump there. Then enable motion and turn in place a little (↶ ↷): __ROBOT__ only refines its estimate while moving. Confirm when the pink laser dots match the walls: at least 40% must lie on map walls (a right pose usually gives 50–80%, a wrong one 10–20%) and the autopilot must say accurate. Furniture that is not on the map lowers the score. Auto-fit searches around the yellow arrow (or the green estimate) for the best laser fit; it only moves the yellow arrow, you still check and send it.





Step 3 · Enable motion




Before the real base may move, check:


  • The area around __ROBOT__ is clear
  • The physical e-stop is tested and within reach
  • The arm is stowed
  • Onboard jobs and scheduling are disabled
  • You stay with the robot and supervise it




Manual drive (right column or arrow keys) works once enabled, even without localization.
